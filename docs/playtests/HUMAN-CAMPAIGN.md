# Consented human playtest campaign — not yet started

Target30 complete games:10 two-player,5 three-player,8 four-player and7 five-player. At least6 sessions include novices, across player counts; at least5 sessions use Danish. Recruit adults with varied negotiation/board-game experience and include keyboard/accessibility needs. No testers have been recruited or contacted and no consent, feedback or completed session is fabricated.

## Before each session

Record the exact private build commit, rules version, scenario seed, firm/seat assignment and tutorial version. Counterbalance firms and seats rather than seating the same specialist first. Explain that this is an experimental fictional game about arms trading and political consequences. Participation and any recording are optional; a participant may stop without explanation. Obtain consent separately for play observation, anonymous metrics and any audio/video/quotation. No personal names, emails, recordings or credentials go into this repository. Keep any contact/consent identifiers in the organizer’s approved private location. Agree retention/deletion and quotation approval before recording; do not silently opt players into analytics.

EN invitation copy: “Would you like to test an unfinished fictional negotiation board game? The session may take up to two hours. We will observe rule clarity, meaningful decisions and negotiation. Participation is voluntary; recording and anonymous metrics require separate consent. No purchase is involved.”

DA invitation copy: “Vil du afprøve et ufærdigt, fiktivt forhandlingsbrætspil? Afsæt op til to timer. Vi undersøger reglernes klarhed, meningsfulde valg og forhandling. Deltagelse er frivillig; optagelse og anonyme målinger kræver særskilt samtykke. Du skal ikke købe noget.”

These are prepared copy, not sent messages. Participant coordination and consent must be real before a session is counted.

## Observation protocol

1. Start comprehension timing when the tutorial begins. Ask the player to explain their first legal decision and its cost; record elapsed time and assistance rather than declaring a10-minute pass from a UI click.
2. Play every round using the actual browser. Observe normal negotiation; do not script a “successful” deal for the record. Record round lengths, longest wait without a decision, proposals/acceptance/withdrawal, shared contract outcomes, political counterplay, cash/debt, investigation and perceived choices. Pause for wellbeing or technical problems; retain pause time separately.
3. Mark every ambiguous rule and every moment requiring facilitator interpretation. Keep exact anonymized wording and state/seed/replay location. A facilitator explanation resolves that session, not the underlying product defect.
4. Finish the full game or mark it abandoned/incomplete with reason. Only a complete8-round session counts toward30 full games. Record observed winner, firm, seat and initial initiative separately.
5. Ask each person independently: Which decision mattered most? When did you lack a useful choice? Was cooperation attractive without being mandatory? Could you respond to unrest? Did the leader policy change your plan? Which rule/art/text was unclear? Would you choose to play again, and why?
6. Record anchored1–5 responses for decision agency, downtime, negotiation usefulness, rule clarity and willingness to replay, plus open feedback. Ratings are exploratory, not automatic evidence of fun.

Two-player sessions separately investigate credible outside offers, coalition incentives and forced cooperation. Four/five-player sessions emphasize queueing, repeated leader/bid interruptions, kingmaking and exclusion. Novice sessions separately measure learning and distinguish rules knowledge from interface discovery.

## Iteration and acceptance

Review each batch of5; prioritize exploit/ambiguity/blocking-access defects. Keep data by exact rules/build; do not pool different revisions as if identical. Rerun changed mechanics with the affected cohort. Median90–120minutes, first-turn comprehension≤10minutes and zero ambiguities in final blind tests are targets, not current measurements. A final blind test has no rules coaching. Thirty sessions do not override negative evidence or the owner’s final experience approval.
