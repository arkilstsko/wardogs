# Designvalidering v1.0

29 kontroller passerede, inklusive 12.000 isolerede egenskabstilfælde. Det er ikke 12.000 gennemspillede kampe.

- 10,000 largest-remainder pools conserve money/prestige
- Five participants cannot duplicate a three-point pool
- Catalogue has 180 stable IDs
- Twelve families
- Every good has eight tender designs
- Intro tender destinations are active
- Ten objective deals fit twelve physical cards
- Bank component sum is 1000 M
- 2,000 random 2–5-player starter drafts conserve all four 12-unit pools
- External delay grants one grace extension
- Second delay gives no second deadline extension
- Final-round delay cannot create round 9
- Six-round scenario delay cannot create round 7
- Restructuring instant wealth gain is below its final penalty
- Round-trip buy/sell alone creates no money
- Underground purchase followed by ordinary resale has no cash-only profit
- Round 1 uses exactly the shared action pools
- Round 2 uses exactly the shared action pools
- Worked example round 3 cash
- Worked example shared prestige is bounded
- Three actual shipments remain in transit
- Delayed GB contract has one grace extension
- No resources created during two rounds
- Black market moves one existing unit without changing the quoted price
- An exhausted black offer cannot be bought twice
- Eight-round continuation settles all remaining shipments
- Meridian completes an earned objective in the continuation
- New final ledger is source-calculated from transactions
- At a six-M offer active delivery beats passive saving in isolated probe

## Økonomiske stikprøver

Passiv formue: 9 point. Kontorarbejde hver runde: 14 point. Syv lovlige leverancer ved tilbud 1/3/6 M: [19, 24, 31] point under de udtrykkeligt forenklede antagelser.

## Menneskelig spiltest

- Blind read: players set up without designer intervention. Record ambiguous steps.
- At least two tables each at 2, 3, 4 and 5 players; all four scenarios across the sessions.
- Record game duration, negotiation duration, winners by firm, office actions, mean final score, tender bid spread and contract completion rate.
- Track whether standard/express/gray all receive informed use and whether insurance has a meaningful decision.
- Require participants to explain liability and prestige allocation before their first five-party agreement.
- Test last-round delay, sanctioned arrival, a lost signature race, debt cap, empty contact deck and a repeat deadline extension.
- Collect agency/fairness ratings for late incidents; replace an incident if several players report no meaningful counterplay.
- Do not call the game balanced from the arithmetic probes.
