## Immediate research risks

- Full primary-rules review is incomplete; several publisher PDFs failed to fetch, and no cited video was watched. The matrix records these limits explicitly.
- The latest physical Root law may differ from the digital build reviewed in 2020. Do not infer digital parity.
- Publisher descriptions and reviewer opinions are directional evidence, not controlled comparative studies.
- No actual China factory quotes were requested or received by this research task. Draft RFQs may be prepared, but sending them requires explicit authority to contact recipients.
- No trademark search, legal clearance, human recruitment, participant consent, accessibility certification, performance certification or factory readiness is claimed.
