# Research matrix — experimental rules, not frozen

Research accessed 2026-10-25 UTC; written 2026-10-26 UTC. This is an evidence register, not a claim that the mandatory research gate passed. Searches, publisher pages and readable excerpts were inspected. Several primary PDFs could not be retrieved; video listings were located but their videos were not watched. No source art, text, card names, boards or branding is licensed for reuse by this research.

**Evidence labels:** P = publisher/designer primary source; R = independent review; L = located only; U = attempted but unavailable; I = our design inference. A readable rule excerpt is not a complete rules review. The nine-game primary-rules plus reputable-review requirement remains OPEN.

## Originality and rights

The candidate contribution is the combination of fictional trading houses, country-specific fictional succession, resource escrow, separately allocated consortium revenue/liability and investigation consequences in a physically representable tender game. This is a design claim, not a novelty search, legal opinion or trademark clearance. Generic mechanisms inform experiments; protected expressive elements must not be copied. Maintain original card text, icon source files, leader names and illustrations, geographic provenance, font licenses and commissioned-art agreements. The working title and fictional organization/leader names require clearance before release.

## Open research gate

1. Obtain/read the complete publisher-authorized rules for every title, including digital-specific rule changes and exact editions. Close failed PDF retrievals with accessible copies; do not treat search snippets as completed reading.
2. Inspect substantive reputable reviews for Sheriff 2e and Dune Imperium; watch cited videos only if accessible and record timestamps/claims. Complete inspection of Brass and Root/Wingspan primary rules.
3. Add independent work on negotiation bots and economic loop testing; present current decisions as hypotheses.
4. Verify font licenses and exact geographic data actually shipped; this matrix does not certify assets.
5. Conduct consented human sessions and physical bookkeeping tests. No reviewed game or automated win-rate result establishes this game's fun, clarity, duration or manufacturability.
