## Design Context

### Users
Two to five people playing a substantial negotiation board game together, locally or in a private browser room. The user explicitly wants a tactile tabletop rather than a dashboard.

### Brand Personality
Restrained, material, satirical. Humor concerns corporate greed, paperwork and responsibility; fictional people occupy real countries.

### Aesthetic Direction
Follow the existing Figma file xt8J8b4flEVpe1tAX36I6w, pages 10–14. Petrol felt, parchment cards, brass edges, original company seals, Phosphor Duotone symbols. Barlow Condensed, Inter and Source Serif 4 are established product fonts. References supplied by the user include John Company, Brass Birmingham, Pax Pamir, Scythe, Arcs and Dune Imperium; their protected artwork and layouts are not assets.

### Design Principles
1. Keep the geographical board central and physical cards recognisable.
2. Make commitments, deadlines and actual costs explicit before consent.
3. Reveal detailed rules and card prose in readable folios.
4. Keep private information outside other players' online projections.
5. Support keyboard operation, reduced motion, and the two required desktop sizes.
